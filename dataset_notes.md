# Synthetic dataset notes

`ecommerce_sales.csv` is generated by `scripts/generate_dataset.py` with NumPy seed `20261018`. It contains 800 order-line rows dated January through September 2026. The names and transactions are fictional; no real customer or company data is used.

## Financial rules

- `Unit_Price` is the listed price per unit.
- `Discount` is a fraction (for example, `0.10` means 10% off).
- `Revenue = Quantity × Unit_Price × (1 − Discount)`, rounded to two decimal places.
- `Cost` is quantity multiplied by the product's synthetic per-unit cost, also rounded to two decimals.
- `Profit = Revenue − Cost`, rounded to two decimals.
- Order status is descriptive. The financial fields show the order-line amount before any status-based accounting adjustment; cancelled and returned rows are not zeroed out.

Each row is one product line/order, with a unique `Order_ID`; customers may appear on multiple rows to represent repeat purchases. Re-running the script overwrites the generated CSV with the same data.
